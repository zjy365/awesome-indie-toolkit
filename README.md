# Awesome Indie Stack

> Your complete technology arsenal for building and scaling products as an indie developer.

## What's This About?

Building digital products solo? This collection brings together the most powerful tools and frameworks that successful indie developers rely on daily. From AI-powered coding assistants to payment processors, from deployment platforms to design tools - everything you need is here.

**Why these tools?**

- ⚡ **Speed**: Ship faster with battle-tested solutions
- 💰 **Value**: Free tiers and indie-friendly pricing
- 🚀 **Growth**: Scales from MVP to millions of users
- 🌍 **Global**: Works anywhere, serves everyone

**Join the community!** Submit PRs to share your favorite tools or open issues to suggest improvements.
